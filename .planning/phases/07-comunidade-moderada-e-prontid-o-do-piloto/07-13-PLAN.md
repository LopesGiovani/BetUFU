## Inline Execution Extension

Continuous owner Phase7 execution/test/self-healing/local-commit authority applies
on master, without worktrees, Antigravity or push. PLAN and
docs/phase7-decisions.json are supplemental bookkeeping paths. Candidate inspection
is delegated to the executor; no separate human digest/policy reply is fabricated.

Mute applies to every chat channel of the account. Preference writes use an
account-wide monotonic version; stale expectedVersion returns409 and requires
refetch. Retries never silently overwrite another device. Failed synchronization
does not claim persistence; the later UI may apply its immediate local overlay.
GET/PUT/DELETE return the private full account list and its version. PUT accepts
only expectedVersion; DELETE supplies the quoted canonical version in If-Match.
Every mute response supplies ETag with that version.

Reports use canonical Idempotency-Key UUID plus reason-only JSON. One immutable
report per reporter/message is canonical; same reason/new key creates only an
immutable receipt, and conflicting reason or key reuse returns409. Different
reporters never merge their private identities or trigger a sanction. A removed
message may still be reported by an authorized authenticated reader; the response
contains only reportId/reportedAt. Current session and publication access are
checked after account locking; report/mute never check write eligibility/guards.
