## Security And Privacy Checks

- The suite uses the hosted Keycloak form and server-side session cookies; it
  does not inject cookies, tokens, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or auth
  state.
- Business APIs are not intercepted with `page.route`, `context.route` or
  `route.fulfill`.
- Credentials are read at runtime from the approved local seed documentation
  into environment variables and are not versioned or printed.
- Traces, screenshots and videos are disabled. Storage assertions inspect only
  key names and reject auth/token markers without reading sensitive values.
- The Playwright base URL is restricted to loopback.
