# Plan 05.1-05 Summary

Aligned the public betting ticket with the market's canonical close deadline
while preserving server-side authority and accepted receipt recovery.

## Delivered

- Added `AppClock`, an injectable clock with a cancellable `scheduleAt`
  boundary. Production uses browser time; tests control the instant without
  waiting for real time to pass.
- `PublicMarketBettingStore` now derives unavailability from both market state
  and `closeDeadline <= now`. Invalid deadlines fail closed.
- The store schedules one active transition for an OPEN market, cancels it on
  reconfiguration/destruction and verifies market identity before applying it.
- Availability precedence is explicit: accepted receipt, suspended/closed or
  expired market, then visitor, enrollment, balance and form states.
- Authenticated users can still recover their own accepted receipt after the
  market closes, without loading enrollment or credit balance for a new bet.
- Submission performs a final client-side deadline check immediately before
  POST. The Spring `PredictionService` remains the authoritative race-safe
  validation for state and time.

## Boundary And UX Properties

- Visitors see an expired or suspended market instead of an unnecessary login
  invitation.
- At the exact deadline instant, the form is absent and `canSubmit` is false.
- A page left open transitions through a Signal update in the zoneless app.
- A previously accepted ticket remains the dominant state both during the
  timed transition and after page refresh.
- Client time is only a usability guard; manipulated clocks cannot authorize a
  bet because the backend revalidates its own `Clock` inside the transaction.
